FAQ

Answers up front

Is it really free?

Yes. The free plan stays free: the page, request form, stats and QR code are included. Pro is for more links, themes and stats history.

Do I need a password?

No. You sign in with a one-time email code that works for 10 minutes. Failed attempts are limited.

Do you track my visitors?

No. Views and clicks are counted without cookies or visitor IP addresses. Bots are excluded.

Where do form requests go?

To the Requests tab and to your email. Pro can export them to CSV.

Can I change my page address?

Yes, in the profile. The old address is released, so update the link in your social bio.

How do I pay for Pro?

By card or SBP through YooKassa. There is no subscription: Pro works for the paid period and does not renew by itself.
