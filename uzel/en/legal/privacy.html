Privacy Policy

What Uzel collects, why, where it is stored and how you can control it.

Version of 15 November 2026.

This policy explains what personal data Uzel (the "Service") processes, for what purpose and on what basis, where it is stored, for how long, and what rights you have. The data controller is Geserd (geserd.com, the "Operator", "we"). The policy follows Russian Federal Law No. 152-FZ of 27 July 2006 "On Personal Data" and, for users in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

We try to collect as little as possible: the Service is built without advertising trackers, without cookies for statistics and without selling data.

1. Whose data is processed in what role

Three groups of people meet in the Service, and their data is handled differently:

  • Users: account owners. For their data the Operator is the controller.
  • Visitors: people who simply open someone's page. We collect almost nothing about them (see section 4).
  • People who submit a request through a form on someone else's page. For this data the controller is the page owner and the Operator acts as processor on the owner's instructions (see "Processing of Visitor Data").

2. Data we process about users

Account data

  • email address, name (if you provide one), interface language, account creation date and last sign-in date, plan status and expiry.

Page content

  • page name and subtitle, description, photo, page address, appearance, links, social profiles, music, request form settings, link schedule. You decide to make all of this public.

Sign-in and security data

  • a hash of the one-time sign-in code (the code itself is not stored) and the number of failed attempts; for the life of a session: a hash of the session token, browser and device type (user agent), IP address, and the times the session was created and last active. In the sessions list the IP is shown in shortened form.

Payments

  • order number, plan, amount, status and date. Bank card details are entered on the YooKassa side; we do not see or store them. A receipt is sent to your email.

Enquiries

  • correspondence with support at support@antviz.ru and the information you give in it.

3. Form requests and complaints

  • Requests: name, contact (email, phone or messenger), message text, date, and the sender's technical IP address (used only to protect against spam and to limit submission rate). The request is stored in the page owner's dashboard and emailed to them.
  • Complaints about pages: the reason and explanation you give. The reporter's data is not saved and we do not record your IP address with a complaint.

4. What we collect about page visitors

When someone opens a page of a Service user, we record only anonymised events: that the page was viewed, that a specific link was clicked, the domain of the referring site (for example instagram.com), the device type (phone or desktop) and the time. We do not use cookies for statistics, do not record IP addresses, do not create visitor identifiers and do not track visitors across sites. The "Do Not Track" (DNT) signal is honoured. Bots are excluded from statistics. A specific person cannot be identified from this data.

If a visitor submits a request, section 3 applies.

5. Purposes and legal bases

  • Creating and running your account, sign-in, publishing your page, sending sign-in codes, request notifications and the weekly summary: performance of the Terms with you (Art. 6(1) item 5 of 152-FZ; Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Showing statistics to the owner, the QR code, data export: performance of the Terms.
  • Taking payments, issuing receipts, accounting: performance of the Terms and legal requirements (Art. 6(1)(b), (c) GDPR).
  • Protection against abuse, spam and fraud, investigating complaints, security: our legitimate interest and the interest of other users (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; Art. 6(1) item 7 of 152-FZ).
  • Replying to enquiries and support: performance of the Terms and legitimate interest.
  • Complying with the law and requests of authorised bodies: legal obligation.
  • We do not send marketing emails or news about new features without your separate consent. The weekly statistics summary can be turned off in the Security section.

We do not make decisions that produce legal effects for you solely by automated means, and we do not sell personal data.

6. Who receives data

Data is shared only with those needed to run the Service and only to the extent needed:

  • the payment service YooKassa: processing of payments and receipts;
  • the hosting provider on whose servers the Service runs and the email delivery provider;
  • the DNS and content delivery network (CDN) provider: the Service's traffic passes through its network; storage of personal data in its systems is not intended;
  • public authorities: on a justified request within the law;
  • recipients of your information by your own choice: the page owner receives a request, visitors see the public page.

These companies act on our instruction or on their own behalf within the law and are obliged to keep data secure. We do not share data with advertising networks or data brokers.

7. Where data is stored

Users' personal data is stored in a database on servers in the Russian Federation. If you are outside Russia, by using the Service you understand that your data is transferred to and processed in Russia. For users in the EEA and the UK this transfer is based on its necessity for performing the Terms (Art. 49(1)(b) GDPR) and on your informed consent at sign-up, and we apply the technical and organisational measures listed in section 9. If servers appear in other countries, we will update this policy and tell you in advance.

8. How long we keep data

  • Account and page: while the account exists. After you delete the account the page, photo, requests and statistics are deleted immediately; data leaves backups in their scheduled rotation.
  • Sign-in codes: deleted one day after they expire.
  • Sessions: deleted when they expire or when you end them manually.
  • Statistics events (views and clicks): no longer than 400 days; in the dashboard you see the period your plan provides.
  • Requests: until you delete them or delete your account.
  • Payment records and receipts: for the period required by accounting and tax law.
  • Support correspondence: up to three years after the last enquiry, so we can handle repeat questions.
  • Server security logs: for the limited time needed to investigate incidents.

9. How we protect data

  • All traffic is sent over a secure connection (HTTPS).
  • One-time codes and session tokens are stored only as hashes. The session is held in a cookie with HttpOnly and SameSite flags.
  • Sign-in attempts and form submissions are rate-limited; there is protection against automated submissions and links to internal networks.
  • Access by staff and contractors is limited to the minimum necessary.
  • Photos are re-encoded and hidden metadata is removed.
  • We keep software up to date and make backups.

In case of a breach creating a risk to your rights, we will notify you and the competent authorities within the periods set by law (in the Russian Federation: Roskomnadzor within 24 hours of the incident and within 72 hours of the results of the internal investigation; under GDPR: the supervisory authority within 72 hours).

10. Your rights

You can at any time:

  • find out whether we process your data and get a copy (a JSON data export is available in the Security section);
  • correct inaccurate data (name, description and links are changed in the dashboard, the email address is changed on request);
  • delete your account and data in the Security section or on request to support@antviz.ru;
  • restrict processing and object to processing based on legitimate interest;
  • receive your data in a structured form and transfer it to another provider (portability);
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent: withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before it;
  • complain to a supervisory authority: Roskomnadzor (rkn.gov.ru) or the data protection authority where you live in the EEA or the UK.

We respond to requests within 30 days (in the EEA the period can be extended under GDPR rules where necessary). To make sure a request comes from you, we may ask you to confirm it from the account's email address. For data in requests submitted on other people's pages, contact the page owner: they decide what happens to such a request, and we will help delete it.

11. Cookies and local storage

The Service uses only necessary technical means: a session cookie (after sign-in) and a record of your chosen language in the browser's local storage. There are no analytics or advertising cookies. See the Cookie Policy.

12. Children

The Service is not intended for people under 16. If you believe a child under that age has created an account, write to support@antviz.ru and we will delete the data.

13. Links to third-party sites

Users' pages contain links to other sites, embedded music and other materials the Operator does not control. Their privacy policies apply independently of ours.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The new version is published on this page with a new date; we will tell you about material changes in advance by email or in the dashboard. Previous versions are available on request.

15. Contact

For any data questions: support@antviz.ru. We answer both data subject requests and questions about how the Service works.
